# Polish list

Things in the app that feel clunky, collected before fixing. Newest at the bottom. When the list is complete, we rank it and fix it as one build.

| # | What's clunky | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Undo after adding a player | An undo already exists (a small "undo" link under a swapped-in card, plus "↶ Undo last" in the plan bar), but it wasn't noticed in use, so it needs to be much more obvious. Example: a clear undo button in the stats panel right after a swap. |
| 2 | DefCon explanation repeated in every player's panel | The long "DefCon chance = …" paragraph (and the clean-sheet one for goalkeepers) shows every time a player is clicked. Move it to one separate explainer, shown once (e.g. a small "What's DefCon?" panel or info icon), and keep the player panel to the numbers. **Resolved 26 Oct 2026 —** the long paragraph is gone; the new xP breakdown rows give each player's DefCon and clean-sheet chance with a one-line reason instead. |
| 3 | "Suggested sell" box reads as if you should sell the incoming player | The red "↘ Suggested sell" box shows the player to BRING IN under a sell heading, so it looks like that player is the one to sell. Reword and recolour, e.g. "Sell him → bring in Vušković" with the incoming player in green, or a heading like "Suggested swap: Hall out, Vušković in". |
| 4 | xP looks like it has no basis | xP is FPL's own "expected points" (matches exactly for all 305 players checked on 26 Oct), chosen because it beat our models in the backtest (1.37 vs 1.82 error). But FPL's number is mostly recent form, so it looks like "last week's points", and the site never says where it comes from. Label it (e.g. "xP = FPL's prediction, mostly recent form + fixture"), link to the backtest, and consider showing our own v2 estimate next to it so there's visible reasoning. **Resolved 26 Oct 2026 —** xP now comes from our own v2 model, and the player panel shows its breakdown (playing, goals and assists, clean sheet, DefCon) with a reason for each part and an amber "bonus points not included" warning. |
| 5 | **(Top priority, not just polish)** xP is really FPL's "form", and the backtest that chose it may be flawed | Checked live FPL data 26 Oct: FPL's ep_next equals its `form` (avg points over ~30 days) for 412 of 451 players, so the xP shown is a recent average, not a forecast. Yet the backtest says FPL's prediction (1.37 error) beat v2 (1.82) and a last-5 average (1.93), which a form number shouldn't do by that margin. Suspect the archive's FPL-prediction column was captured after matches (leakage). Check the backtest; if confirmed, switch the site's xP to the v2 model, rewrite the backtest result and the interview explainer, and label xP clearly. Also: the live site was a full gameweek behind (GW8 finished, data saved 23 Oct) — see roadmap #10. **Checked 26 Oct — leak confirmed:** archive `ep_pre` for week g matches the 4-GW points average *including* week g (MAE 0.66 / 0.59 for 2024-25 / 2025-26) far better than the average before it (1.02 / 1.10); players who scored 12+ jump from ep ≈ 3.6–4.2 the week before to ≈ 7.2–7.5 in that same week. On GW8 2026-27, using FPL's ep saved before kick-off (23 Oct) and GW8 results from the live API: v2 MAE 2.11, last-5 average 2.39, FPL ep 2.61 (298 players who played); 1.71 / 2.26 / 2.07 over all 422. One gameweek only, but v2 wins. **Resolved 26 Oct 2026 —** pre-deadline FPL predictions only; the backtest no longer scores the archive's FPL numbers and has a "Leak check" section and a fair "FPL's prediction, saved before kick-off" section (GW8, 427 players: v2 1.832, FPL 2.057; "too early to call" under 5 gameweeks); the site's xP is v2, chosen by the clean backtest; README, explainer and handoff rewritten. The live site being a gameweek behind is still roadmap #10. |
